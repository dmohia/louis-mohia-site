Welcome to my blog! This is where I'll be sharing my thoughts, learnings, and experiences in the world of web development and digital transformation.

## Why this blog?

After several years of helping businesses with their digital projects, I decided to create this space to:

- **Share my knowledge**: The web is constantly evolving, and I learn new things every day that I want to pass on.
- **Document my journey**: Writing helps me structure my thinking and deepen my understanding of topics.
- **Build connections**: I hope these articles will be useful to you and can open up enriching discussions.

## Topics I'll cover

Here you'll find articles about:

1. Modern web technologies (Astro, React, Node.js...)
2. Development best practices
3. Automation and no-code tools
4. Digital strategy for businesses
